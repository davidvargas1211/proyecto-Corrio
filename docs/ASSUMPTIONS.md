# Assumptions and limitations

## Supported by the challenge brief

- The product concerns cross-border B2B payments.
- The target scenario is UAE businesses paying suppliers in India.
- The conversion event is a payment-assessment enquiry.
- Funding and recipient flows are fiat-facing; stablecoins may be internal infrastructure.
- AED/USD funding and INR receipt are used as an illustrative prototype scenario.

## Deliberately not represented as fact

- Pricing, FX rate, FX markup, fees, rate lock, timing, payout rail, cutoff, limits or supplier-bank coverage.
- Legal entity, licensing, regulation, protection, certifications, partner banks, testimonials or customer scale.
- A real payment manager, communication channel, support hours, tracking, payment approval or transfer completion.

## Prototype limitations

The form is local-only. It is not a contact form and does not send or retain user data. A production product would require validated operational rules, legal/compliance review, backend security, consent, data handling, analytics governance and real customer research.
